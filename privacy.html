Privacy Policy

Home Dashboard is a personal, single-user project. Its only user is its owner.

When the owner signs in with Google, the dashboard reads their Google Calendar so it can show upcoming events on their own screens. It uses the access only for that.

  • Calendar data is read on demand and shown on the owner's screens. It is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • The sign-in is stored only on the owner's own computers and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

Home Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
